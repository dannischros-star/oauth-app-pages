个人自用 OAuth 应用 / Personal-use OAuth App

本应用仅供其所有者本人在自己的电脑上使用，用于自动化处理所有者本人的 Google 账号数据（例如给自己发送每日摘要邮件并核验发送结果）。本应用不向公众提供服务，不接受其他用户注册或使用。

This application is used solely by its owner, on the owner's own computer, to automate tasks on the owner's own Google account (for example, sending a daily digest email to themselves and verifying that it was sent). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
